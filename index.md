# Privacy Policy for Chor Police (चोर पुलिस)

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

Chor Police is an offline pass-and-play party game. This policy explains
what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing.

## Information we collect

None. Chor Police does not collect, store on any server, or share any
personal information.

- **No accounts.** You never sign up or log in.
- **No internet access.** The app does not request the internet
  permission and makes no network connections.
- **No ads and no analytics.** There are no advertising or tracking
  libraries in the app.
- **No device permissions.** The app does not ask for your location,
  contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Information stored on your device

The player names you type and your game settings (language, region,
timers, sound and accessibility choices) are saved only on your own
phone, so they are there the next time you open the app. This
information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with
**Settings › Reset settings**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Children

Chor Police does not knowingly collect any information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page
with a new "Last updated" date.
